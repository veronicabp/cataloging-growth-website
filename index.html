03 / ABOUT THE RESEARCH

Data & methodology

A brief overview of how the preferred hedonic series are constructed.

01

Historical catalog data

We digitize approximately 5.1 million product listings from Sears catalogs spanning 1900–1990. Language models extract product prices, descriptions, and characteristics such as size, materials, and functional features.

02

Hedonic quality adjustment

Within product categories, ridge regressions relate log prices to characteristics using rolling five-year windows. We identify closely comparable products across years and use estimated characteristic values to adjust prices when products differ in quality.

03

Price index construction

Quality-adjusted price relatives are combined within goods categories, then aggregated using historical consumer expenditure weights. The resulting series track price changes holding measured product quality as comparable as possible.

Interpretation

The indexes measure changes in quality-adjusted prices, not prices of individual identical models or the average price of products offered. Individual goods may enter the sample later than 1900. Missing observations are omitted, not filled with zeroes.

Research citation

Bäcker-Peral, Verónica, and Benjamin Wittenbrink. Cataloging Growth: A Re-Evaluation of 1900–1990. Working paper.
